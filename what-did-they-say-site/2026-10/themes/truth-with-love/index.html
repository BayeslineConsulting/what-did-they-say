Truth with love: peacemaking, unity, and boundaries

Sunday's speakers converged on a two-part ethic: forgo contention and seek unity, while not trading truth for approval.

What this theme is about

Kungu's talk is the most operational in the corpus: one list of phrases to retire and one to use. Gilbert supplies the procedure (avoid contentious settings, find common ground, be a light, hold the truth, stay anchored in Christ) and models it with his own failure.
