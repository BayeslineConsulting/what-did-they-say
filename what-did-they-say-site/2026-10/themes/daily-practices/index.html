Theme 8 of 10

Small consistent practices and guarded attention

Small, consistent practices are framed as protective, and their neglect, often through digital distraction, as the mechanism of gradual drift.

What this theme is about

Digital risk recurs across three sessions: mobile gambling (Christofferson), "artificial information" (Andersen), coarse online speech (Cook), sleep and phone limits (Muñoz Spannaus). Practices are the means, never the message, which is why the theme is broad but thin.
