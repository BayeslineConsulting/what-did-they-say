Witness and invitation

Testimony is democratized: every member is cast as a witness whose simple words carry power, anchored to apostolic witness and an invitation extended to all people.

What this theme is about

Witness runs from the top down (apostolic witness, a family that sailed through a storm to hear an apostle) and from the bottom up (a niece, a Primary child, a 25-minute Sunday School lesson). Oaks extends the invitation to all nations and frames grace as universal but conditional.
