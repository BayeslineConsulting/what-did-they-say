Divine identity and unearned love over labels

Identity as a child of God, and a love that cannot be earned or lost, is offered as the antidote to worldly labels, paired with a warning that love is not license.

What this theme is about

The boundary arrives inside the same talk as the love. Folley warns against the lie that "because God loves us, we can be saved regardless of our pride," anticipating Sunday's truth-with-love ethic.
