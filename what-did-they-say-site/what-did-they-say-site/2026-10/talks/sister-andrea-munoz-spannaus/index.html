Sunday afternoon session · October 2026 General Conference

Sister Andrea Muñoz Spannaus

God Is at Your Side

Young Women General Presidency

Read the full talk on churchofjesuschrist.orgWatch on YouTube

In brief

Speaking to young women, Sister Muñoz Spannaus recalls making a list of aspirations as a girl after studying the 13th article of faith, and likens the Lord to a guide who knows our exact location, as her husband did when her GPS failed. She offers seven practices and invites each young woman to choose one: partake of the sacrament humbly, repent daily, study the scriptures, learn about ministering angels, protect your sleep, limit phone use, and seek friends striving to follow the Lord. She closes by inviting them to write their own list of aspirations.
