Sunday morning session · October 2026 General Conference

Elder Quentin L. Cook

Integrity and Virtue Lead to Happiness

Quorum of the Twelve

Read the full talk on churchofjesuschrist.orgWatch on YouTube

In brief

Elder Cook teaches that integrity and virtue sustain disciples on the covenant path, recalling the inscription that inspired young David O. McKay: "Whate'er thou art, act well thy part." He links virtue and happiness, noting the Declaration of Independence's 250th anniversary, and condemns coarse language and online bullying as violating the Savior's teachings. Repentance is part of integrity; a World War II veteran he taught as a young missionary compared forgiveness to stepping onto the soil of home.
