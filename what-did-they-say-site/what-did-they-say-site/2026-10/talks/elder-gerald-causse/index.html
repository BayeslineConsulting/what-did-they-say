Saturday morning session · October 2026 General Conference

Elder Gérald Caussé

The Gift of Testimony

Quorum of the Twelve

Read the full talk on churchofjesuschrist.orgWatch on YouTube

In brief

Elder Caussé, recalling the scripture that comforted him at two calls to serve, teaches that bearing witness of Jesus Christ belongs not only to apostles but to every disciple who has taken His name by covenant. A testimony need not be long or eloquent, only true; his niece's two-sentence testimony at camp is his example. He invites everyone to look daily for brief, natural chances to share their witness, linking "I know" and "I love you" as expressions of the same gift.
