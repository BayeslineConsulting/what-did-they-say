Sunday afternoon session · October 2026 General Conference

President Dallin H. Oaks

Closing Remarks

President of the Church

Read the full talk on churchofjesuschrist.orgWatch on YouTube

In brief

In his brief closing remarks, President Oaks invites everyone to study and ponder the conference talks prayerfully and to act in faith on the principles they teach. He announces that the Salt Lake Temple will open to the public on April 5, 2027, points to TempleSquare.org and the Temple Square app, and mentions a new video series premiering after the session. He blesses members with peace and testifies that truth and the covenant path are found in the restored Church of Jesus Christ.
