Sunday morning session · October 2026 General Conference

Elder Alfred Kyungu

Using the Language of Peacemakers

Seventy

Read the full talk on churchofjesuschrist.orgWatch on YouTube

In brief

Elder Kyungu takes up President Oaks's April 2026 invitation to forgo contention and use the language of peacemakers. Citing Proverbs, the Savior, and Paul, he gives concrete examples of words that wound, such as "you never listen" or "this is all your fault," and words that build peace, such as "I'm sorry," "I forgive you," and "let us start again." He invites listeners to repent of harsh words, fill their hearts with Christ's doctrine, and become instruments of His peace.
