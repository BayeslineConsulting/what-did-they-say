Saturday morning session · October 2026 General Conference

President D. Todd Christofferson

O Be Wise

First Presidency

Read the full talk on churchofjesuschrist.orgWatch on YouTube

In brief

President Christofferson takes up a subject he calls a duty to address: gambling. He describes how smartphones have turned it into a constant, private activity, cites survey figures showing especially heavy participation among teenage boys and young adults, and explains that the industry's revenue is gamblers' losses. Quoting President Oaks's question "Where will this lead?" and Paul's warning about becoming a stumbling block, he calls gambling morally wrong because it takes without giving, and pleads with members to refrain and use the time and money for good.
