Saturday morning session · October 2026 General Conference

Bishop Sean Douglas

The Hour of Your Mission Is Come

Presiding Bishopric

Read the full talk on churchofjesuschrist.orgWatch on YouTube

In brief

Bishop Douglas thanks missionaries of every age and urges the rising generation to place full-time missionary service high on their list. He tells of Elder Merlin Sanchez in Peru, who returned to church at 25 with the help of friends, served faithfully, and now leads a family and a stake. He restates that worthy, able young men are asked to prepare and serve, that a mission is a powerful option for young women to pray about, and that there is still room for everyone.
