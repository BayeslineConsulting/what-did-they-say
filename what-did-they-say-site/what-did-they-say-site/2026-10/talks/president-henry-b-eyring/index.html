In brief

President Eyring testifies that the promises in the hymn "How Firm a Foundation" are true: the Lord does not remove our trials, but He is with us in them. His mother, who taught him that life is meant to be a test, endured lifelong illness with faith, while his father cared for her and thanked every nurse who helped. Quoting President Kimball that God usually meets our needs through another person, he thanks those who pray for and reach out to others, encourage missionaries, and sustain leaders, and invites more to join them.
