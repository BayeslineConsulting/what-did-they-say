Elder Paul H. Sinclair

Quick to Help, Slow to Judge

Seventy

Read the full talk on churchofjesuschrist.orgWatch on YouTube

In brief

Elder Sinclair recalls a Primary boy who buttoned his sleeve to his lapel and got stuck, and the little girl who quietly freed him while he himself felt only irritation, a moment that taught him to be quick to help and slow to judge. He finds the same pattern in the Savior's treatment of the woman at the well, whom He met with dignity and living water rather than shame. Citing President Oaks's call to seek to be holy like the Savior, he invites members to help new members and friends in more Christlike ways.
