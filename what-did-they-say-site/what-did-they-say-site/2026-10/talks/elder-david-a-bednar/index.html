In brief

Elder Bednar, using the image of a damaged passport rejected at a border, teaches that ordinances and covenants are our credentials for returning to God, and that their integrity must be maintained through ongoing faithfulness, ratified over time by the Holy Spirit of Promise. He explains that a temple sealing has both a marriage aspect and a vertical tie to God's eternal family. To a faithful spouse whose partner breaks covenants, his answer is an emphatic no: another person's choices cannot deprive a covenant keeper of any promised blessing.
