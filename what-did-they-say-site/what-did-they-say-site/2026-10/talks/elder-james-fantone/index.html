Sunday morning session · October 2026 General Conference

Elder James G. O. Fantone

Take Upon Us His Name

Seventy

Read the full talk on churchofjesuschrist.orgWatch on YouTube

In brief

Elder Fantone recalls signing his own name on his daughter's school form, then fixing it by simply adding her first name, and contrasts that quick fix with what it means to take upon us the name of Christ. Taking His name is not adding a badge but a lifelong determination to serve Him, which King Benjamin teaches we do by serving others. His parents, who gave their children's bedrooms to a student and to children needing a safe home, and homesick missionaries who came to love the work, show how service changes the server.
