Saturday morning session · October 2026 General Conference

Sister Tamara W. Runia

The Sculpting of Our Souls

Young Women General Presidency

Read the full talk on churchofjesuschrist.orgWatch on YouTube

In brief

Sister Runia shares the sudden death of her son Ryan and the lesson she drew from Joseph Smith in Liberty Jail: hard experiences can be for our good. She retires the idea that the gospel is an umbrella that keeps the rain off, describing it instead as the solid rock we stand on in the storm, and teaches that not understanding is not the same as not believing. Her brother's sculpting class illustrates how the Savior, once invited, shapes us into what we could not become alone.
