Saturday afternoon session · October 2026 General Conference

Elder Ronald A. Rasband

Love Like Jesus

Quorum of the Twelve

Read the full talk on churchofjesuschrist.orgWatch on YouTube

In brief

Elder Rasband speaks to everyone grieving for loved ones who have turned away from the Lord, acknowledging that lecturing and pressure often widen the rift. He asks them to turn their despair over to Jesus Christ, who loves the wanderer even more and has already paid for their choices, and to lead with love, as Alma's father did in praying for his son. His three suggestions: let go of guilt and never give up, never let a problem matter more than a person, and remember that Christ is the answer.
