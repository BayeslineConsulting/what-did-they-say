Sunday morning session · October 2026 General Conference

Elder Neil L. Andersen

Courage Born of Our Faith in Jesus Christ

Quorum of the Twelve

Read the full talk on churchofjesuschrist.orgWatch on YouTube

In brief

Elder Andersen welcomes President Rosemary Chibota, who joined the Church at 17 after reading a pamphlet about Joseph Smith, and teaches that spiritual courage, born of faith in Jesus Christ, has always been required of disciples, from Moroni to today. Contrasting his quiet boyhood days herding cows with the constant noise of phones and "artificial information," he urges stillness to nourish faith. Examples range from a young President Oaks accepting a demanding calling to Spokane fire survivors who said their faith was secure, and he promises that strengthening faith brings answers and increased spiritual power.
