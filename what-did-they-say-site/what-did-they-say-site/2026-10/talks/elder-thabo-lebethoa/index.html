Sunday afternoon session · October 2026 General Conference

Elder Thabo Lebethoa

Jesus Christ Is the Light and Life of the World

Seventy

Read the full talk on churchofjesuschrist.orgWatch on YouTube

In brief

Elder Lebethoa, whose name means "joy" and whose wife's means "eternal," recalls their first serious argument as newlyweds and how the Savior's words that contention is not of Him showed them the way forward. Whatever our struggles, he testifies, the answer is Jesus Christ, the light of the world. He suggests three ways to receive more of His light: strengthen faith in Christ, study the scriptures daily, especially the Book of Mormon, and practice daily repentance.
