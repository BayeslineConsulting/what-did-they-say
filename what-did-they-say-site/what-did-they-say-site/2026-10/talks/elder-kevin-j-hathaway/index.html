Saturday morning session · October 2026 General Conference

Elder Kevin J. Hathaway

Jesus Christ Is the Great Deliverer!

Seventy

Read the full talk on churchofjesuschrist.orgWatch on YouTube

In brief

Elder Hathaway testifies that Jesus Christ is the great deliverer and that the question for the faithful is not whether He will deliver, but how and when. Drawing on a highway accident at the start of his mission leadership and a missionary who lost her father a week before returning home, he outlines a pattern: opposition raises hard questions, sincere seeking brings assurance, grace enables us to endure, and deliverance comes in the Savior's time and way, some now, some after waiting, and some in the Resurrection.
