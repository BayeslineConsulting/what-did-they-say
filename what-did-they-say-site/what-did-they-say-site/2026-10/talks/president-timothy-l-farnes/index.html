Saturday afternoon session · October 2026 General Conference

President Timothy L. Farnes

Divine Appointments

Young Men General President

Read the full talk on churchofjesuschrist.orgWatch on YouTube

In brief

President Farnes recalls a discouraging day as a young priesthood leader when then-Elder Dallin H. Oaks noticed him in the temple, listened, and lifted him. Years later President Oaks did not remember it, but replied that "it's what we do." Linking the sacrament covenant to daily ministering, and telling of helping a frightened new missionary through his first flight, he invites listeners to pray each day for eyes to see the "divine appointments" God places in their path.
