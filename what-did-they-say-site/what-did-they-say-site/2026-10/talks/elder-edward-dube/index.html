Sunday morning session · October 2026 General Conference

Elder Edward Dube

Come unto Christ

Presidency of the Seventy

Read the full talk on churchofjesuschrist.orgWatch on YouTube

In brief

Elder Dube recalls that in 1987 then-Elder Oaks had a "Trespassers will be prosecuted" sign removed from a mission office gate, teaching him that all are invited to come unto Christ. From Moroni he draws three truths: come, deny yourselves of ungodliness, and be perfected in Him, since we come to Christ to be perfected rather than perfecting ourselves first. A brother struggling to quit smoking set a temple sealing date and was sealed two months later, and Elder Dube invites each listener to help one person come unto Christ.
