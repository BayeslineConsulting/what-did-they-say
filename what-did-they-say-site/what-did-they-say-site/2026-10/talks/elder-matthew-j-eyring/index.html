Saturday afternoon session · October 2026 General Conference

Elder Matthew J. Eyring

Comfort from the Holy Ghost

Seventy

Read the full talk on churchofjesuschrist.orgWatch on YouTube

In brief

Elder Eyring speaks to the moments when we realize "I can't fix this," the thought that came to a friend whose son had died. He shares how he and his wife, who lost one of their twins before birth, comforted a young couple facing the same loss. Self-reliance eventually meets burdens no one can carry alone, and the Savior, who knows our feelings, comforts us especially through the Holy Ghost. Quoting President Oaks on the literal, universal resurrection, he urges listeners to make the constant companionship of the Holy Ghost one of their highest priorities.
