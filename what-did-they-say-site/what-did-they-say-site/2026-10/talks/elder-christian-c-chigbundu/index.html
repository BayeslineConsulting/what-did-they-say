Elder Christian C. Chigbundu

Spiritual Immunity

Seventy

Read the full talk on churchofjesuschrist.orgWatch on YouTube

In brief

Elder Chigbundu, recalling the talk of "immunity" during the COVID-19 pandemic, describes building spiritual immunity through four small and simple things: scripture study, prayer, the sacrament, and keeping covenants, including temple worship. He tells of a homesick new missionary steadied by studying the Book of Mormon and a sister with cancer who felt the Savior's love through daily prayer. These practices, he teaches, are the armor of God that helps us withstand life's challenges.
