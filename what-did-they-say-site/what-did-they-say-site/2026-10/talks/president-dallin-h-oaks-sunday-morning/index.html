Sunday morning session · October 2026 General Conference

President Dallin H. Oaks

The Gospel of Jesus Christ Is for All

President of the Church

Read the full talk on churchofjesuschrist.orgWatch on YouTube

In brief

President Oaks testifies that this is Christ's Church and that the way through every struggle, including doubt, is to draw closer to Jesus Christ through daily prayer and scripture study, with patience for others and for ourselves. He urges members to keep their commandments and covenants, protected by the sacrament and temple, and teaches that there is no ultimate conflict between scientific and spiritual ways of knowing. Citing the Book of Mormon's witness that God's grace is for all people, he calls on everyone to act on these truths and share them.
