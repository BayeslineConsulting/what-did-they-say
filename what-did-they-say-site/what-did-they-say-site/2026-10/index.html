What did they say in the last general conference?

Here is one careful answer: every talk summarized, the patterns we noticed across all 37, and everything we were explicitly asked to do, starting with President Oaks.

The 5-minute version

37 talks · 10 themes · 34 invitations
A pattern worth pondering

Christ as the one who carries and lifts was the pattern we noticed in the most talks: 19 of 37.

The arc

Saturday dwelt on trials (77% of the adversity material); Sunday turned to how we treat each other (90% of the truth-with-love material).

What the prophet asked

President Oaks asked us to focus on developing faith in Christ, hold on to the first article of faith, keep our commandments and covenants, protect ourselves through the sacrament, believe the Book of Mormon's promises, act on and share these truths, and study the talks.

The surprise

"Love" in its forms was spoken 270 times, nearly double "covenant" (148).
