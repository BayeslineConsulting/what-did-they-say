October 2026 General Conference · October to March

Your six-month plan

28 direct invitations from this conference, in the speakers' own words, starting with President Oaks. Choose one to three, turn each into a specific commitment, and check in each week until the next conference.

Choose what to work on

Only direct invitations are listed: sentences where a speaker said “I invite,” “let us,” “please,” “I urge,” “I plead,” or the like. Each is shown in the speaker's exact words. Why turn one into a “when” and a “what”? People who decide exactly when and how they will act are much more likely to follow through than people who only intend to (Gollwitzer & Sheeran, 2006).
