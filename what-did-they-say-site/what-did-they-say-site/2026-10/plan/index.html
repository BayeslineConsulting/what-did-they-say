October 2026 General Conference · October to March

Your six-month plan

34 things speakers explicitly asked us to do, starting with President Oaks. Choose a few, make each one specific, and check in each week until the next conference.

Choose what to work on

Why decide “when” and “what” in advance? Research on habits finds that people who plan exactly when and how they will act are much more likely to follow through than people who only intend to (Gollwitzer & Sheeran, 2006). Small, specific, and checked weekly beats big and vague.
