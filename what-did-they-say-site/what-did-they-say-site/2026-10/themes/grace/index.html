Christ as the active agent: grace that lifts and carries

Christ, not the self, is cast as the agent of change (carrying, lifting, sculpting), and receiving that help requires setting aside pride and self-reliance.

What this theme is about

Grace works as connective tissue: the turn inside talks about adversity, wayward children, and teaching rather than a topic of its own. Sister Runia's sculptor and Elder Dunn's friend who quietly carried the pack make the same move: help resisted at first, then recognized.
