A giving economy: sacrificial laws vs something for nothing

Saturday set sacrificial giving laws (fasting, offerings, tithing) against gambling's take-without-giving logic, recasting money as a test of trust and neighbor-love.

What this theme is about

Soares on fasting and Christofferson on gambling sat back to back on Saturday morning. Christofferson's is the only data-driven talk. The two tithing talks split the promise: Jufra stresses the faith test, Villanueva the discipline of noticing blessings already received.

What we were asked to do
