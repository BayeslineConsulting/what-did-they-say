Covenants as living, protective, generational power

Covenants are presented as active power that binds the keeper to Christ, reaches across generations, and cannot be voided by another person's choices.

What this theme is about

Covenants are described as acting when the person is not: ancestors' covenants reach a descendant raised elsewhere, and "covenants do not take a day off" (Morgan quoting Oaks). The protective claim runs both ways, for the wandering child and for the faithful spouse.
