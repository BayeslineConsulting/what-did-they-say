Covenants as living, protective, generational power

Covenants are presented as active power that binds the keeper to Christ, reaches across generations, and cannot be voided by another person's choices.

What this theme is about

Covenants are described as acting when the person is not: ancestors' covenants reach a descendant raised in another church, and covenants "do not 'take a day off'" (Elder Morgan, quoting President Oaks). The protection runs both ways, for the wandering child and for the faithful spouse, and three talks tie the weekly sacrament to staying on that path.

Direct invitations in this theme

Keep the commandments and your covenants“I urge you to keep the commandments and be true to the covenants so many of you have made to guide you along the covenant path.”Asked by President Oaks
Receive the sacrament with purpose“let us not miss the unifying power of the sacrament”Asked by President Uchtdorf
Honor the temple with gratitude“Please let us never speak of a temple as big or small, elaborate or plain.”Asked by Elder Gong
Be a strong link“in the chain of your eternal family, please be a strong link”Asked by Elder Gong
