Small consistent practices and guarded attention

Small, consistent practices are framed as protective, and their neglect, often through digital distraction, as the mechanism of gradual drift.

What this theme is about

Digital risk recurs across three sessions: mobile gambling (President Christofferson), "artificial information" (Elder Andersen), coarse online speech (Elder Cook), sleep and phone limits (Sister Muñoz Spannaus). Practices are the means, never the message, which is why this theme reaches 24 talks but with few passages in each.
