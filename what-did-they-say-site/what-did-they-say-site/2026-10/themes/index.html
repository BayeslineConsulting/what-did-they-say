October 2026 General Conference

Ten themes we noticed

One careful way of organizing what was said, found by coding all 808 passages and grouping the codes. They are lenses for pondering, not an official or final summary; the talks themselves are always the source.

How these themes were found →

Theme 1Covenants as living, protective, generational power

Covenants are presented as active power that binds the keeper to Christ, reaches across generations, and cannot be voided by another person's choices.

16 of 37 talks · 5 invitations
Theme 2Adversity reframed: purposeful, not prevented

Speakers corrected a prosperity reading of obedience: faithfulness does not prevent trial, trial is purposeful, and deliverance comes in God's time and way.

15 of 37 talks · 2 invitations
Theme 3Christ as the active agent: grace that lifts and carries

Christ, not the self, is cast as the agent of change (carrying, lifting, sculpting), and receiving that help requires setting aside pride and self-reliance.

19 of 37 talks · 2 invitations
Theme 4Discipleship as striving, not prior perfection

Discipleship is framed as sustained trying rather than a perfection threshold, which lowers the felt cost of failing.

15 of 37 talks · 4 invitations
Theme 5Seeing and ministering to the one

God's promise to succor is described as fulfilled mainly through ordinary people who first notice the individual, so ministry starts with seeing.

14 of 37 talks · 2 invitations
Theme 6Divine identity and unearned love over labels

Identity as a child of God, and a love that cannot be earned or lost, is offered as the antidote to worldly labels, paired with a warning that love is not license.

10 of 37 talks · 1 invitations
Theme 7Truth with love: peacemaking, unity, and boundaries

Sunday's speakers converged on a two-part ethic: forgo contention and seek unity, while not trading truth for approval.

11 of 37 talks · 4 invitations
Theme 8Small consistent practices and guarded attention

Small, consistent practices are framed as protective, and their neglect, often through digital distraction, as the mechanism of gradual drift.

15 of 37 talks · 6 invitations
Theme 9A giving economy: sacrificial laws vs something for nothing

Saturday set sacrificial giving laws (fasting, offerings, tithing) against gambling's take-without-giving logic, recasting money as a test of trust and neighbor-love.

6 of 37 talks · 2 invitations
Theme 10Witness and invitation

Testimony is democratized: every member is cast as a witness whose simple words carry power, anchored to apostolic witness and an invitation extended to all people.

17 of 37 talks · 6 invitations
