Witness and invitation

Testimony is democratized: every member is cast as a witness whose simple words carry power, anchored to apostolic witness and an invitation extended to all people.

What this theme is about

Witness runs from the top down (apostolic witness, a family that sailed through a storm to hear an Apostle) and from the bottom up (a niece's two sentences, a Primary child's song, a 25-minute Sunday School lesson). President Oaks extends the invitation to all nations and teaches that grace is for all, on conditions the Savior has set. This was the pattern we noticed in the most talks: 25 of 37.

Direct invitations in this theme

Believe the Book of Mormon's promises“These promises from the Book of Mormon are true. Millions believe them and have had experiences to affirm their truth. I invite all to believe them.”Asked by President Oaks
Act on these truths and share them“I call upon all to act on these truths and to share them with others that we may all draw closer to Jesus Christ, our Savior.”Asked by President Oaks, Elder Stevenson
Bear your witness of Christ“I invite you to look each day for opportunities, as prompted by the Holy Ghost, to bear witness of Jesus Christ and His restored gospel”Asked by Elder Caussé
Come unto Christ“I invite you, please come unto Christ and embrace His promise”Asked by Elder Dube
Invite one person“My humble invitation to us all: Will you find one person and invite him or her to come unto Christ by exercising faith to follow His teachings, or invite a friend to meet with the missionaries?”Asked by Elder Dube
Love the Lord and those you teach“these two great commandments become one simple invitation: Love the Lord and love those you teach.”Asked by Brother Reid
Put a mission high on your list“To all the rising generation of the Church, I invite you to place serving a full-time mission high on your list.”Asked by Bishop Douglas
