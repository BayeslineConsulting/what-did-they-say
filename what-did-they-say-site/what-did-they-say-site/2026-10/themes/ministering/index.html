Seeing and ministering to the one

God's promise to succor is described as fulfilled mainly through ordinary people who first notice the individual, so ministry starts with seeing.

“it is usually through another person that he meets our needs”

President Eyring · T19.13, T20.08

What this theme is about

In every helping story the sequence is notice, then act: the frightened missionary at the airport, the janitor whose name was known, the child who unbuttoned a sleeve. President Eyring and President Chibota quoted the same line from President Kimball in the same session; the text cannot show whether that was planned.
