Seeing and ministering to the one

God's promise to succor is described as fulfilled mainly through ordinary people who first notice the individual, so ministry starts with seeing.

What this theme is about

In every helping story the sequence is notice, then act: the frightened missionary at the airport, the janitor whose name was known, the child unbuttoning a sleeve. Two speakers cited the same Kimball line in one session, a notable convergence, though the transcript cannot show whether it was planned.

What we were asked to do
