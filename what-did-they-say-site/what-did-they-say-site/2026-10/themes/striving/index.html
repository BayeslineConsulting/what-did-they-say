Discipleship as striving, not prior perfection

Discipleship is framed as sustained trying rather than a perfection threshold, which lowers the felt cost of failing.

“We do not need to perfect ourselves to come to Christ.”

Elder Dube · T23.10

What this theme is about

Failure is relocated from the outcome to quitting. Elder Dube's story inverts the usual sequence: the couple set a sealing date before the husband had quit smoking. Most of this theme fell in the Sunday morning session, where Elder Andersen's talk on spiritual courage and President Oaks's "We are all a work in progress" sat side by side.
